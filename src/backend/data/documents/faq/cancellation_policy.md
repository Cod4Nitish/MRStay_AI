# Cancellation Policy
If a guest cancels within 24 hours of booking, they receive a full refund. 
If they cancel within 7 days of the stay, they receive a 50% refund.
Any cancellation less than 7 days before the stay is non-refundable.

# Check-in and Check-out
Check-in time is 3:00 PM. Check-out time is 11:00 AM.
Late check-out may be requested but is subject to availability and a fee of $50.
